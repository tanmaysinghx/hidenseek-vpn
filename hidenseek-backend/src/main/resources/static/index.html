Features

Everything a VPN should be.
Nothing it shouldn't.

No ads, no data brokers, no "unlimited" plans that aren't. Just a tunnel that works, on hardware you know.

WireGuard fast

A modern protocol built into the Linux kernel. Connects in under a second and stays out of your way on Wi-Fi and mobile data.

Full tunnel, no leaks

All IPv4 and IPv6 traffic goes through the tunnel, and DNS is forced through the server, so lookups never reach your ISP.

Keys stay on your device

Each device makes its own key pair. The private key never leaves it, not even to our server.

Pick your location

Every server the admin adds becomes a location on the map. Switch with one tap; the tunnel moves with you.

Clear daily plans

See exactly how much data you've used today and when it resets. Need more? Request a bigger plan from the app.

One account, every device

Sign in on your laptop and phone with the same account. New people are approved by an admin before they can connect.

Install guide

  1. Run hidenseek-vpn-windows-x64-setup.exe. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (the app isn't code-signed yet).
  2. Open Hidenseek VPN from the Start menu and accept the administrator prompt; it needs this to manage the tunnel.
  3. Sign in and tap connect. The first connect sets up the WireGuard engine automatically.
  1. Ubuntu / Debian: double-click the .deb to install it in Software, or:
    sudo apt install ./hidenseek-vpn-linux-amd64.deb
  2. Fedora:
    sudo dnf install ./hidenseek-vpn-linux-x86_64.rpm
  3. Arch / CachyOS / Manjaro:
    sudo pacman -U hidenseek-vpn-linux-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst
  4. Open Hidenseek VPN from your app menu, sign in and tap connect. That's it: WireGuard is built into Linux, and no password is asked when you connect.
  5. Other distros: the AppImage runs anywhere without installing (it asks for your password when connecting).
    chmod +x hidenseek-vpn-linux-x86_64.AppImage && ./hidenseek-vpn-linux-x86_64.AppImage
  1. Download hidenseek-vpn-android.apk on your phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Hidenseek VPN and sign in.
  4. Tap connect and accept Android's VPN connection request (only the first time).
Privacy

Straight answers about your data

A VPN moves trust from your ISP to the server's operator. Here is exactly what the operator of this server can and can't see.

Never visible

  • Your private key. It never leaves your device.
  • The contents of HTTPS pages, messages, passwords or full URLs. They're encrypted end to end.
  • Anything you do while disconnected.

What the server records

  • Your account email, device name, connection times and data used.
  • The public IP you connected from and its approximate location (country, state, city).
  • Account actions made through the apps and this site, with the IP and location they came from, and pages viewed on the console. Kept for 90 days.
  • Domain names you look up, only on servers where the admin has turned on activity logging. Kept for 30 days.

Run your own Hidenseek

Got a VPS? Deploy the backend with Docker, add servers with one command, and invite your friends. Every location is a box you control.
